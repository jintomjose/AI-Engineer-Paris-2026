Before we start

Let's open with a laugh.

Kitze on what it is really like to be an AI engineer today.
Paris · 23–24 September 2026

Two days. One idea kept coming back.

Rooms full of people who build with AI every day. Hosted by Mistral.
The one big idea

AI agents are becoming our users, our buyers and our workforce.

So we have to design for them, feed them great context, and govern them.
What I heard

Shift 1 · Customer

Agents are the new users.

UX, CX, DevX. Paris added a fourth persona: Agent Experience (AX).
Most billing documents are already read by AI agents, not humans.
Chart shown in the Stripe talk, AI Engineer Paris
Shift 1 · Customer

And they are starting to buy.

Agents now initiate payments. The rules of the agent-payments economy are not written yet.
Shift 2 · Build

Everyone has agents. Nobody has orchestration.

Context is everything. An agent is only as good as what it is given.
Great data is critical. Without it, slop grows.
Specialists beat generalists. Many agents, one common core.
Shift 3 · Control

We learned to observe our software. Now we must observe our workforce of agents.

Hottest topic in Paris
Agent observability.
Agent sprawl is real
Many companies no longer know what their agents do, or where.
Track and fence
Track every agent and its work. Run agents inside guardrails.
Shift 4 · Market

Models converge. Pricing moves to outcomes.

Consumption: per token, per call
Workflow: per task completed
Outcome: per result delivered
Also: sovereign AI is a real option, and everyone is building infra.
We have been here before

The technology is ahead. The organisations are not, yet.

Computers took a decade to show up in productivity. The unlock was software, skills, processes and organisational capital.
What I'm taking home

The model is becoming a commodity. The advantage is context, data and discipline.

"The actual AI engineer builds the system around the agents."
